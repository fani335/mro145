MRO 145 Aviacomponent

Maintenance and repair of aviation equipment, components and units.

01 About us

What we do

MRO 145 AVIACOMPONENT performs maintenance and repair of aviation equipment — primarily aviation components and units of the hydraulic, fuel and pneumatic systems.

Work on every item follows the same route: intake, incoming inspection and fault analysis, agreement of the scope of work with the customer, repair, bench testing and handover of the unit with accompanying documents.

A separate line of work is arranging export and repair of components abroad, when the work has to be performed by the manufacturer or by a specialised service centre.

03 Approach

How we handle an item

Three principles that define the order of work at the shop floor.

01 / Transparency

Scope agreed in advance

A repair starts only after fault analysis and after the list of work, the cost and the timeline have been agreed with the customer.

02 / Control

Checks at every stage

Incoming inspection at intake, control during assembly and bench testing before the item is handed over.

03 / Documents

The item is handed over with documents

Each unit comes with a set of accompanying documentation covering the work performed and the test results.

04 Equipment

Equipment and personnel

The work is carried out on new equipment by specialists with extensive experience in component repair. Our test equipment allows the unit parameters to be verified after repair without sending the item elsewhere.
