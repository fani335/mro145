Questions and answers

We have collected what customers ask most often before submitting an item for repair. If your question is not here, call us or leave a request.

We accept aviation components and units of the hydraulic, fuel and pneumatic systems: pumps and hydraulic motors, cylinders and actuators, valves and distributors, filters, monitoring instruments, high-pressure hoses. If your type of item is not on this list, call us and we will check the specific position.

The timeline depends on the type of item, its condition and the availability of the required parts. We give exact timelines after incoming inspection and fault analysis — before disassembly the scope of work cannot be estimated reliably. The agreed timeline is recorded together with the scope of work and the cost.

The cost consists of the scope of restoration work and the cost of the parts being replaced. It is determined from the fault analysis results and agreed with the customer before the repair starts. We do not begin work without that agreement.

The item goes through a visual examination and a completeness check, and is then disassembled. The condition of the assemblies is assessed and it is determined what has to be replaced and what can be restored. The resulting list of work is sent to the customer for approval.

A set of accompanying documentation is prepared for the unit: the list of work performed, details of replaced parts and the bench test results. The exact composition of the set is confirmed when the contract is signed — it depends on the type of item and the customer's requirements.
