All services in detail

04 Scope

Items we work with

We accept components and units of the hydraulic, fuel and pneumatic systems of an aircraft.

  • Pumps, hydraulic motors and pump stations
  • Hydraulic cylinders, actuators and drive mechanisms
  • Valves, distributors and control fittings
  • Filters, filter elements and cleaning components
  • Pressure gauges, pressure sensors and monitoring instruments
  • High-pressure hoses, pipelines and connections

05 Workflow

How a repair proceeds

Four stages — from request to handover of the item.

Stage 01

Request

We take your enquiry, clarify the type of item and the nature of the fault, and agree the handover terms.

Stage 02

Intake and inspection

We carry out incoming inspection and disassembly, determine the condition, scope of work and cost.

Stage 03

Repair

We perform restoration work and replace parts within the agreed scope, keeping records for every item.

Stage 04

Testing and handover

We verify the unit parameters on the bench, prepare the documents and hand the item over to the customer.
