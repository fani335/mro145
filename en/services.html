Our services

A complete cycle of work with an aviation component — from intake and fault analysis to bench testing and handover of the item with accompanying documentation.

01

Component maintenance and repair

We accept aviation components and units, perform maintenance, restoration repair and serviceability checks. Every item is tracked separately: what arrived, what the inspection revealed, and what work was carried out.

  • Intake of the item and handover paperwork
  • Disassembly, flushing and cleaning of assemblies
  • Replacement of worn parts and seals
  • Assembly and adjustment of the unit
  • Serviceability check and document preparation
02

Repair of hydraulic units

We work with units and elements of the aircraft hydraulic system. After repair, the unit is verified on a bench for pressure, flow rate and tightness parameters.

  • Pumps, hydraulic motors and pump stations
  • Hydraulic cylinders, actuators and drive mechanisms
  • Distributors, valves and control fittings
  • Filters, filter elements and cleaning components
  • High-pressure hoses, pipelines and connections
03

Fuel and pneumatic units

We service and repair fuel system units and pneumatic system elements. Particular attention goes to the tightness of connections and the accuracy of control element actuation.

  • Fuel system units
  • Pneumatic system elements and pressure reducers
  • Control and shut-off fittings
  • Pressure gauges, pressure sensors and monitoring instruments
04

Incoming inspection and fault analysis

Before we quote timelines and cost, the item undergoes incoming inspection and disassembly. Based on the findings we agree the scope of work with the customer — we do not start a repair without this step.

  • Visual examination and completeness check
  • Disassembly and assessment of the condition of assemblies
  • Determination of the scope of restoration work
  • Agreement of cost and timelines with the customer
05

Bench testing

After repair, the unit undergoes parameter verification on a test bench. The test results are recorded and handed to the customer together with the item.

  • Tightness check
  • Testing under operating pressure
  • Verification of flow and adjustment characteristics
  • Documentation of test results
06

Export and repair of components abroad

Arranging export and repair of components abroad is a separate line of our work. We take care of preparing the shipment, dealing with the service centre and returning the unit to the customer.

  • Selecting a service centre for the specific item
  • Preparing the item and shipping documents
  • Following the work and monitoring timelines
  • Returning the item to the customer
